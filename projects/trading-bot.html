The idea

I wanted to build something real and fully autonomous: a bot that could make its own trading decisions and execute them without me triggering anything, and eventually grow into something that could trade real money and be profitable.

How I built it

I built the bot on Replit and connected it to Alpaca for market data and order execution through its API. I used ChatGPT and Claude to refine and clean up the code and to engineer the prompts and decision logic. It now runs on its own and places trades autonomously. To keep it transparent, I built a custom dashboard that shows the actions it takes, the context behind each automated decision, and the entry and exit of every trade.

My role

Solo project, end to end: the idea, the build (vibe coding), the Alpaca integration, the prompt engineering, the dashboard, and the ongoing iteration as I improve its decisions and risk handling.
