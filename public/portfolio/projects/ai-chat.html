The problem

Knowledge is scattered across data warehouses, wikis, ticketing and document stores. Getting a reliable answer means knowing where to look and how to query it, which slows everyone down and creates a bottleneck on a handful of experts.

The approach

A Retrieval-Augmented Generation assistant, used right inside Slack, that supports both structured querying for analytical reporting and unstructured querying across documents and tickets. It connects to sources like Snowflake, Jira, Confluence and SharePoint, retrieves the relevant context, and returns a grounded answer with its sources, so anyone in the company can self-serve.

My role

I led this as the product manager from idea to launch. I ran discovery, defined the query types and the in-Slack experience, prioritized the source connectors, and shaped the retrieval and grounding approach. I built in feedback loops to keep improving accuracy, relevance and user satisfaction, and drove adoption across teams.
