The problem

Compliance posture data is scattered across various teams, with no unified way to comprehend it. Different personas spend time just figuring out what the data is even trying to say, which makes the whole process slow and inefficient.

The approach

ComplyScope reframes compliance as observability: one source of truth, three role-based lenses, and narrative summaries that turn raw metrics into what is improving, what is at risk, and what to do next.

My role

I owned this end to end, from idea to reality. I interviewed and aligned stakeholders on the problem and the goals, then shaped that into a PRD. I designed the experience in Figma, aligned with tech and dev leads on feasibility, ran POCs, and coordinated dependency teams through delivery, tracking scope, risks and dependencies along the way.

Role-based views

Leadership

High-level posture, trend, audit readiness and top risks, with no noise. Includes an executive summary.

Team Lead

Where is my pod’s posture, and where does it need to be? Score vs. target, control areas and open items.

PM / Dev

The detailed work: what is asked, why it matters, who owns it and when it is due, fully filterable.

Insights

An insight feed plus per-area audit readiness: readiness score, evidence freshness and timeline.
