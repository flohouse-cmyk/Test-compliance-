The problem

Contact-center agents spend a large share of their time on repetitive, low-complexity call types. That volume buries the calls that genuinely need human judgment and empathy, drives up handle times, and makes it hard to scale support.

The approach

A natural-language voice agent that authenticates the caller, detects intent, and handles routine, nuanced call types end to end. On inbound, it fields calls from prescribers, members, pharmacists and third-party vendors, resolving routine ones autonomously and escalating to the call center team when a person is needed. On outbound, it proactively reaches members and prescriber offices in support of internal pharmacists and pharmacy techs. Every interaction is logged to Salesforce, so there is a clean case record and a smooth handoff when a person takes over.
