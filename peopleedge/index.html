Frequently Asked Questions

Engagements vary by scope. Talent acquisition projects typically run 8–16 weeks. HR strategy and organizational design projects average 3–6 months. Culture and DEI transformation programs are typically 12-month partnerships with ongoing support.

We partner with organizations of all sizes, from 50-person scale-ups to 50,000+ employee enterprises. Our service packages are scoped to match company stage, budget, and HR maturity — so you get the right level of support without paying for what you don't need.

We have deep expertise across technology, financial services, healthcare, professional services, manufacturing, and retail. Our consultants are matched to engagements based on industry background, ensuring relevant experience from day one.

We establish clear KPIs during the discovery phase — specific to your business — such as time-to-hire, offer acceptance rate, employee NPS, attrition rate, or cost-per-hire. Monthly reviews track progress against baselines and we adjust strategy in real time.
