About

The experience behind PeopleEdge

Veteran-Owned · SDVOSB

Hi, I'm Ashley — founder of PeopleEdge.

For more than 12 years, I've worked across the full spectrum of HR and talent acquisition — recruiting for hard-to-fill roles, building compliant hiring processes, designing intern and early-career programs, and coaching individuals through career pivots and job searches.

I've seen firsthand that great companies are built on great people decisions — and that most small and mid-size businesses simply don't have access to senior HR expertise when they need it. PeopleEdge changes that. You get a seasoned partner who does the work alongside you, scaled to your size and budget.

As a veteran-owned, SDVOSB-certified practice, I'm especially proud to serve government contractors and mission-driven employers — bringing the compliance rigor and the human care that the people side of business deserves.

What "veteran-owned, SDVOSB" means for you

PeopleEdge is a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business. For government contractors and federal buyers, that means we're eligible for SDVOSB set-aside work — helping you meet small-business and veteran subcontracting goals while getting senior HR expertise. For every client, it means a partner built on discipline, integrity, and follow-through.
