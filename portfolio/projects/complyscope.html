The problem

Compliance posture is usually buried in spreadsheets and audit binders — leadership can’t see risk at a glance, team leads don’t know where their pod stands versus target, and delivery teams lack a clear, prioritized list of what to fix and why. Everyone is looking at different numbers.

The approach

ComplyScope reframes compliance as observability: one source of truth, three role-based lenses, and AI-style narrative summaries that turn raw metrics into “what’s improving, what’s at risk, and what to do next.” It ships with realistic sample data across 5 frameworks and 6 delivery pods so the story is presentation-ready.

My role

This project grew out of work I led at Microsoft on a transparency security & compliance-posture platform. I drove the product vision, the role-based information architecture, the scoring/readiness model and the end-to-end experience design.

Highlights

Role-based views

Leadership

High-level posture, trend, audit readiness and top risks — no noise. Includes an AI executive summary.

Team Lead

“Where is my pod’s posture, and where does it need to be?” Score vs. target, control areas and open items.

PM / Dev

The detailed work: what is asked, why it matters, who owns it and when it’s due — fully filterable.

Insights

AI insight feed plus per-framework audit readiness: readiness score, evidence freshness and timeline.

Frameworks

A searchable control inventory across every framework in scope.
